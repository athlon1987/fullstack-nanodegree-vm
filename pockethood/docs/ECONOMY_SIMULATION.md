# Economy & Flywheel Simulation

_Generated by `npm run economy:sim` (scripts/economy/simulate.ts) on 2026-10-27._

World: **Kampung Seri** (`my.kampung_seri`) with its active modules.

Bots play the **real game engine** for 30 simulated days with a simple, reasonable policy: collect everything, deliver orders, chat daily, start and complete resident stories, follow a fixed build order, upgrade when affordable (keeping a small reserve), keep production slots full (prioritising items needed for orders/stories/upgrades), decorate when comfortable, contribute to the landmark (social profiles), give spare coins and goods to the shared neighbourhood spots (profiles that decorate or socialise, keeping a 500-coin reserve), expand land when wealthy.

**These are pacing estimates under stated assumptions, not retention data.** Real players are less efficient than bots in some ways (they don't optimise) and more in others (they play longer when engaged).
