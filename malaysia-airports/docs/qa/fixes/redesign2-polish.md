# Redesign stage two, polish pass: the critique's must-fixes and ranked improvements

Owner: lead designer-engineer, finishing the redesign before the client sees it. Date: 23 November 2026.
Input: `docs/design/critique-redesign2.md` (6.9 / 10), Direction v2, the stage 1c previews and the Hamad, Dubai and
Changi references. Scope: everything under `src/app/**`, `src/components/**`, `src/app/globals.css`, the i18n
dictionaries (keys appended only) and the end-to-end specs. Another engineer worked in the same folder; nothing
outside these paths was changed, and `src/content/images.ts` was not edited (the place band reuses the existing
`heroForAirport`, so no image choice or focal point changed).
