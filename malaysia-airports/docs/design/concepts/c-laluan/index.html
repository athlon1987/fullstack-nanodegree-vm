Which car park should I use for a week away?

SuggestionFor a week away from KLIA Terminal 1, the Long Term Car Park is the natural choice.

Official informationIt has over 5,200 bays, with a free shuttle to the terminal every 10 minutes. Closer in, Terminal 1 parking has over 5,700 bays across Blocks A to D.
