02Plan your journey

From your door to the gate, laid out plainly

Add a flight and we set out each step in order, and tell you how sure we are of each one. What is an instruction, what is an estimate and what is only a suggestion are never blurred together.

How to read the labels

  • Official instructionFrom the airport, your airline or the authorities. Please follow it.
  • Estimated guidanceOur best estimate from current conditions. It can change.
  • SuggestionA helpful idea for a smoother trip. Take it or leave it.

05Concierge

Ask, and be shown the way

A concierge for the questions that do not fit a menu. It answers in plain language, in English or Bahasa Malaysia, and waits quietly until you need it.

  • Answers come from official airport information, and say so.
  • When it is not sure, it tells you, and points you to a person.

Where can I park for a week away?

The Long Term Car Park has over 5,200 bays, with a free shuttle to the terminal every 10 minutes. For shorter stays, Terminal 1 has over 5,700 bays across Blocks A to D.
