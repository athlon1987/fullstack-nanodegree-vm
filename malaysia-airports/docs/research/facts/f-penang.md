## 2. Traffic and capacity

- The airport handled **8.31 million passengers in 2025** [pen-passengers-2025] (H), up from 7.47 million in 2024 according to Bernama [pen-passengers-2024] (M).
- Sources disagree on the detail: Wikipedia's table shows 8,269,978 for 2025 [pen-passengers-2025-wikipedia-variant] (L), and reported growth rates differ. Quote "about 8.3 million" until MAHB's statistics are checked.
- The existing terminal was designed for **6.5 million passengers a year** [pen-design-capacity] (H), so the airport is running above its design capacity [pen-operating-above-capacity] (H). This is the plain-language reason for the expansion and for peak-time crowding.
- Operating hours: the only statement found is an unofficial "24 hours" on a hotel listing [pen-operating-hours-24h] (L). A CAAM supplement covers scheduled runway closures in force since 28 November 2024 [pen-runway-closure-supplement] (M). Do not publish "open 24 hours" without MAHB confirmation.

## 3. Terminal layout

- One passenger terminal building handles both domestic and international flights [pen-terminal-single-building] (M, prior).
- Arrivals are on the ground level (Level 1), with departures on the level above [pen-terminal-arrivals-ground-level] (M, prior).
- The domestic departures airside area is on the 2nd floor (Level 2), after security clearance [pen-terminal-domestic-departures-level] (M).
- International departures include a mezzanine floor reached near Gate A3 [pen-terminal-international-mezzanine] (M).
- A multi-storey car park stands across the road, opposite the main terminal [pen-terminal-car-park-opposite] (H). A link bridge connects car park Level 3 to the departure level [pen-terminal-link-bridge] (M).
- Car rental counters are on Level 1 of the terminal building [pen-car-rental-counters] (M).

Design note: sources mix "Level 2", "2nd Floor", "Level 3" and "mezzanine" for nearby places. Level naming must be checked on site before it is used in wayfinding copy or maps [pen-terminal-arrivals-ground-level], [pen-lounge-ppl-international-location].

## 12. Terminal expansion

### Scope and targets

- Capacity rises from 6.5 million to **12 million passengers a year** [pen-expansion-capacity-target] (H).
- Aircraft stands increase from 16 to 28 [pen-expansion-aircraft-stands] (M).
- The enlarged terminal is to be built to Malaysia Airports' "Airport 4.0" standard [pen-expansion-airport-4-0] (M).
- A new multi-storey car park roughly doubles parking to about 3,400 bays [pen-parking-expansion-bays] (M).
- Estimated cost: about RM1.55 billion according to Malaysia Airports; one October 2025 headline says RM1.2 billion [pen-expansion-cost] (M).

### Timeline

- Four-year construction period, 2024 to 2028 [pen-expansion-construction-period] (M).
- **Target completion: June 2028** [pen-expansion-completion-target] (H).
- Three phases: relocation of government agency facilities such as the Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia; expansion of the aircraft apron; expansion of the main terminal building [pen-expansion-three-packages] (H).
- Package 1 was awarded to Gagasan Maya in September 2024 (RM107 million) [pen-expansion-package-1-award] (M); Package 2 to Acre Works in December 2024 (RM254 million) [pen-expansion-package-2-award] (M).

### Status in 2026

- March 2026: Phase 1 was 98 per cent complete [pen-expansion-phase-1-progress-mar-2026] (H); the terminal phase had not yet begun [pen-expansion-terminal-works-not-started-mar-2026] (M).
- July 2026: design of the terminal package (WP03) was complete and procurement had started on 25 June 2026 [pen-expansion-wp03-procurement-june-2026] (M).
- The terminal package has slipped against the July 2025 expectation of a contractor by end-2025, while the June 2028 target is still quoted [pen-expansion-wp03-earlier-target-missed] (M). Write "targeted for 2028", not "opens in 2028".
- Some stakeholders expect traffic of 10 to 12 million by 2028; a further expansion is said to be due in 2034 [pen-expansion-capacity-debate] (M).

### Passenger impacts and temporary arrangements found for 2026

- A new e-hailing pick-up zone on the ground floor of the multi-storey car park [pen-ehailing-pickup-zone] (H).
- Airside works under CAAM AIP SUP 32/26: temporary apron and taxiway closures and temporary aircraft stands T1 and T2, for works running 1 March 2025 to 1 July 2027 [pen-expansion-airside-works-supplement] (M).
- Scheduled runway closures under a separate supplement in force since 28 November 2024 [pen-runway-closure-supplement] (M).
- The airport is operating above design capacity during the works [pen-operating-above-capacity] (H).

No source was found for landside or in-terminal temporary arrangements (temporary check-in, gate changes, bussing, kerb or car park closures). That is a gap, not evidence that there are none.
