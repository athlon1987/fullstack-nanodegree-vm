Product implication (a real passenger pain point worth designing for): people park long-term in
the Preferred zone by mistake and get very large bills, and a press report in May 2026 said the
official calculator lagged the new rates. The parking page must make the three products
unmistakably different, show the daily cap first, and warn before a long stay in Preferred.

First-hand facts from the official parking page (retrieved 16 Nov 2026, before access closed):
Terminal 1 Car Park has over 5,700 bays across Blocks A to D; LTCP has more than 5,200 bays and a
complimentary shuttle every 10 minutes; Preferred Parking has over 300 bays on Level 2, Blocks B
and C; features: 24/7 CCTV, air-conditioned waiting lounge, restaurants and cafes nearby,
dedicated bays for families and ladies; online booking with entry and exit date and time.
