## Langkawi (all third-party, conflicting: indicative at most)

- Coupon taxis sold at a counter in arrivals, fixed fares, pay before boarding (several guides agree on the system).
- Fares quoted: to Pantai Cenang RM18 to RM45 depending on the source; to Kuah RM18 to RM25; a 50% surcharge is reported between 23:00 and 07:00. Do not publish a figure; say fares are fixed by coupon and shown at the counter.
- Ride-hailing operates but coverage can be limited at night (guides).
- Car rental is available at the airport (guides quote RM90 to RM120 a day). No public bus on the island (several guides).
- One lounge: Plaza Premium Lounge, reported to have moved within the terminal in early 2026 (one guide).

- Parking and bus details: unverified.

## Product conclusion

The verified outlet directory is small (about ten KLIA entries). The commerce pages must therefore
(a) present what is verified well, (b) say plainly that the directory is partial in this prototype,
(c) link to the official directory for each airport, and (d) show the filter and discovery design
on the data that exists, without padding it with unverified names.
