## 2. Ownership and the 2025 privatisation

- **Consortium.** Gateway Development Alliance (GDA) comprises Khazanah Nasional Berhad, the Employees Provident Fund, the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority and Global Infrastructure Partners [priv-consortium-members, S].
- **Timeline.** Proposal announced May 2024 [priv-2024-05-proposal-announced, S]; cash offer of RM11.00 per share [priv-offer-price, S]; cost of the shares not already held reported at about RM12.3 billion [priv-deal-size, S, headline-derived].
- **Before the deal** Khazanah held about 33.2% and the EPF about 7.9% [priv-pre-deal-shareholding, S].
- **Outcome.** GDA held 98.68% of the issued shares by 17:00 on 12 February 2025 [priv-acceptance-98-68, S]. The shares last traded on 19 February 2025 at RM10.90 and were suspended from 20 February [priv-last-trading-day, S]. The company was removed from Bursa Malaysia's Official List on 25 February 2025 after just over 25 years as a listed company [priv-delisted-2025-02-25, S].
- **Ownership today.** A privately held company wholly owned by GDA, with the Government of Malaysia retaining a special share [ownership-current, S]. The Ministry of Finance (Incorporated) approved the 2026 extension of the Managing Director's tenure [ownership-mof-inc-role, S].
- **Planned stakes.** Under the privatisation plan Khazanah was to hold 40% and the EPF 30% (Malaysian institutions 70%), with GIP and ADIA together holding 30% [priv-stakes-after-deal, S]. This is the split reported in mid-2024, and sources disagree on whether it describes stakes in MAHB or within the consortium. Publish percentages only after official confirmation.
- **Governance effect.** All five independent non-executive directors resigned with effect from the delisting date [governance-ineds-resigned-on-delisting, S].

Design implication: the "Investor Relations" section of a listed company no longer applies. A shareholder/ownership panel and an archive of past annual reports is the appropriate replacement.

## 8. Strategy and major projects

### KLIA Aerotrain replacement (delivered)

- Service resumed on 1 July 2025 after about 28 months [proj-aerotrain-resumed-2025-07-01, S].
- Project cost RM456.1 million [proj-aerotrain-cost, S]; delivered by Alstom with the IJMC-Pestech joint venture [proj-aerotrain-contractors, S].
- Alstom Innovia APM 300R driverless three-car trainsets, up to 270 passengers each [proj-aerotrain-trainsets, S, capacity from one outlet]; journey under three minutes [proj-aerotrain-journey-time, S].
- Returned to round-the-clock operation; more than 6.55 million passengers carried by May 2026 [proj-aerotrain-24h-and-ridership, S, low]. Reports also record service disruptions after the relaunch, so check current operating hours before stating "24 hours".

### KLIA Terminal 1 baggage handling system (in progress)

- Contract awarded by Malaysia Airports (Sepang) to T7 Kemuncak and Siemens Logistics in late 2022 [proj-bhs-contract-award, S].
- Includes a 2,500-position early bag store and a high-speed tray link to the satellite building [proj-bhs-scope, S].
- Completion now targeted for the fourth quarter of 2028 [proj-bhs-completion-2028, S, single source].
